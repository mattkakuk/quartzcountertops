## **How Quartz Compares to Granite and Marble**

Granite and marble are beautiful materials, but each has its own personality.
Marble is elegant but softer and more prone to staining. Granite is strong and
natural, but often needs sealing. Quartz offers a more controlled look and
easier maintenance.

That does not mean quartz is automatically better for everyone. It simply
means quartz is often the more practical choice for homeowners who want beauty
without constant upkeep.

### **Quartz vs. Granite**

Granite is a natural material, which means every slab is unique. Quartz is
engineered, which means the pattern and color can be more consistent. For some
homeowners, that consistency is helpful because it makes design planning
easier.

Quartz also does not require seal simplification, which simplifies it.

### **Quartz vs. Marble**

Marble has a classic luxury look, but it can stain and etch more easily.
Quartz can mimic the appearance of marble while offering a more durable
surface for daily kitchen use.

For families who love the marble look but want fewer worries, quartz is often
the better fit.
