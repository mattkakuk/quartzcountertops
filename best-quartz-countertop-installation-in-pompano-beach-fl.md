### **Quartz vs. Natural Granite**

While granite offers natural, unique mineral patterns, it remains porous and
requires periodic chemical sealing. Quartz provides superior stain resistance
and consistency without recurring maintenance costs.



### **Quartz vs. Marble**

Marble is notoriously soft and susceptible to "etching" from everyday kitchen
acids like lemon juice or vinegar. Quartz gives you the same jaw-dropping
marble visual appeal with none of the delicate chemical vulnerabilities.

## **Essential Maintenance Tips for Your New Quartz Surfaces**

One of the absolute best features of quartz is how simple it is to maintain.
Keep your counters looking brand-new with these basic rules:



### **Daily Cleaning**

Wipe down your counters daily using a soft microfiber cloth, warm water, and
mild dish soap. Avoid harsh chemical cleaners or bleach.



### **Protecting Against Direct Heat**

While quartz is heat-resistant, rapid thermal shocks can cause damage. Always
use trivets or hot pads under hot pans, slow cookers, and air fryers.



### **Handling Accidents**

For stubborn dried food or liquid residue, gently scrape the area using a non-
abrasive plastic putty knife, then wipe clean with soap and water.

### **2\. Can I place hot pots directly on quartz countertops?**

We strongly advise against placing hot pots or pans directly onto quartz
surfaces. While quartz resists moderate heat, extreme temperature changes can
damage the binding resins or cause thermal cracking. Always use trivets or hot
pads.
