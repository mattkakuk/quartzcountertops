## **Essential Maintenance Tips to Keep Your Quartz Pristine**

Engineered stone is incredibly tough, but a little smart care goes a long way
toward keeping your investment looking brand new!

  * **Use Trivets for Hot Pans:** While engineered surfaces handle moderate heat, sudden extreme temperatures (like a red-hot pan straight off the stove) can cause thermal shock or scorch the resin binders.
  * **Always Use Cutting Boards:** Your quartz counters are so hard they will actually dull your expensive knives, but sharp chef knives can still leave slight metal marks.
  * **Clean Up Spills Promptly:** Though stain-resistant, acidic liquids like lemon juice, bleach, or harsh dyes should be wiped away right away.
  * **Avoid Harsh Chemical Cleaners:** Skip harsh oven cleaners, paint strippers, or high-pH solvents. Warm water and mild dish soap are all you ever need!

### **2\. Can quartz countertops be used in outdoor kitchens?**

We generally recommend using quartz for indoor kitchens or covered patio
areas. Prolonged exposure to direct UV sunlight can cause the resin binders in
engineered stone to yellow or fade over time. For outdoor kitchens, we can
recommend specialized UV-resistant engineered series!

### **3\. Will quartz countertops show visible seams?**

Because engineered stone slabs are manufactured in standard sizes, larger
kitchens or L-shaped layouts will require seams. However, our master
fabricators use computerized pattern matching and color-blended epoxy resins
to make seams discreet and virtually invisible.

### **4\. How long does the quartz installation process take from start to
finish?**

Once you select your favorite slab and we complete the digital laser

business days. The actual in-home installation phase is usually finished in

### **5\. Do engineered quartz surfaces scratch easily?**

Not at all! Quartz ranks a 7 on the Mohs Hardness Scale (diamond is a 10),
making it one of the most scratch-resistant
[**countertop**](https://www.google.com/maps?cid=2937615344441069978) surfaces
available today. While it effortlessly resists daily wear, we still recommend
