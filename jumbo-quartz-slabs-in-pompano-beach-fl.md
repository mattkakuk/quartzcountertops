## **Important Things to Consider Before Choosing Jumbo Quartz**

Large quartz slabs need proper support. Cabinets must be secure, level, and

Quartz can look different depending on the lighting. A slab that looks cool in

The right edge should match both the design and the way the kitchen is used.

## **Common Mistakes to Avoid**

Choosing jumbo quartz is exciting, but there are a few things homeowners
should avoid.

### **Choosing Only From a Small Sample**

A small sample does not always show the full pattern. This is especially true
with veined quartz. Larger slabs reveal movement, spacing, and color variation
more clearly.

### **Ignoring the Layout**

The slab needs to fit the design. We always consider island size, cabinet
runs, sink placement, appliance locations, and traffic flow.

### **Forgetting About the Backsplash**

Countertops and backsplashes should work together. A bold slab with a busy
backsplash can feel overwhelming. Sometimes, simplicity is the secret
ingredient.

### **Not Planning for Installation Access**

Jumbo slabs are large, so access matters. A beautiful slab still has to get
inside safely.
