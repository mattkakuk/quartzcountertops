## **Common Mistakes to Avoid When Choosing Quartz**

Quartz is a strong choice, but the selection process still matters. A few
avoidable mistakes can lead to disappointment.

### **Only Choosing Based on a Small Sample**

Small samples are helpful, but they do not always show the full movement of
the slab. If the quartz has veining, larger pieces may look different from
what is expected.

We always recommend viewing the design as clearly as possible before making a
final decision.

### **Ignoring the Backsplash**

The countertop and backsplash sit right next to each other, so they need to
work together. A busy countertop with a busy backsplash can feel overwhelming.

Sometimes the best design choice is balance. Let one feature lead and the
other support it.

### **Forgetting About Lighting**

Lighting changes everything. Natural sunlight, warm bulbs, cool LEDs, and
under-cabinet lighting can all affect how quartz appears.

Before selecting a final color, it helps to think about how the kitchen looks
throughout the day.
